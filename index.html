Personal backup utility

A private backup,
with a clear purpose.

UVic OneDrive Backup is a personal utility for copying selected OneDrive files into the account owner's Google Drive.

This utility is privately operated. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the University of Victoria.

What the utility does

The utility uses rclone and local scripts to inventory files, compare existing content, upload authorized backup files, and create Google Drive shortcuts for additional folder locations. Only files selected by the owner's backup rules are included.

Google authorization lets the utility inspect existing Drive content and maintain the owner's backup. This informational website does not provide a public backup service or a Google sign-in flow.

How data is handled

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Credentials and transfer records are kept in the local backup configuration and working files. No backup files or credentials are published on this website.
